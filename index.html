Now

Vexi has two products, and both are options at heart: one side pays to lock in a price, and the other side gets paid to stand behind it. The Vexi protocol does it on chain for U.S. equity tokens. Basis, built inside Vexi, does it for used GPUs between companies.

Basis

Built inside Vexi · A private market for used GPUs · Started November 2026

Data centers own GPUs that lose value every month, and there's nowhere to hedge it. With Basis, an owner pays a fee to lock in a floor price for selling later, and a buyer that doesn't need the newest chips earns that fee by agreeing to buy at that price.

  • Companies only, each one checked first. Every deal ends with real machines inspected and handed over in the data center.
  • An escrow agent holds and pays the money. Basis never takes a side and never holds client money.
  • Same team as Vexi. Abner and I built the whole product ourselves.
